# The free Phish setlist prediction game

Setlist Pick'Em is a free live **setlist picks game** for fans who love predicting setlists—built first for Phish, and designed as a home for more bands soon. Lock openers, closers, encore, and a wildcard before showtime, then score points for correct picks as the night unfolds.

## What is a setlist prediction game?

For jam bands like Phish, every night is unique, but patterns emerge that help the astute fan anticipate how the setlist will unfold. A setlist prediction game—sometimes called a **fantasy setlist** game—asks you to call songs, and to predict where they will land in the setlist. The goal is simple: prove you have mastered the art of setlist construction, earning points along the way to "put your money where your mouth is" and win the night. You compete with friends in private pools and with everyone on the global leaderboard. Scores update as songs are played, along with a live view of the setlist so you never miss the band's last move, even if you couldn't make it to the venue or carve out time for the live stream. Prefer a short walkthrough? See [how it works](/how-it-works).

Fans have been calling the set on paper, in group chats, and on couch tour for years. Setlist Pick'Em turns that ritual into a live game: lock your setlist picks, watch the show, and climb the board together. We're live with Phish today and building toward more bands soon.

We follow the tour with you. Song frequency, bustouts, and gap highlights live on our [tour stats](/tour-stats) pages and refresh every night the band plays live—insights that help you stay sharp between shows. When you join the community, you'll have access to even more tour data, along with your own personal stats like picking average (think batting average, but for setlist picking accuracy), Bustout Boost™ hits to measure how often you predict catalog rarities, and a heatmap with all of your most frequent picks to hone your setlist prediction skills. 

## Fantasy setlists, without the spreadsheet

Setlist games have always been fun, but just like paper in the early aughts, we've outgrown spreadsheets today. As fans ourselves, we set out to make it easier for our friends to manage the setlist picks game we've been playing for 25 years. We automatically track the points you earn for hitting setlist slots and wildcards, and Bustout Boosts when you call songs that are not heavy in the rotation. Full point values are on [how scoring works](/how-scoring-works).

- **Before the show:** lock picks before the showtime.
- **During the show:** live scoring and standings as the setlist unfolds—at the venue or on couch tour.
- **After the show:** final grades, tour standings, and personal stats that grow every night you play.

Playing unlocks those personal stats—so you can see how you stack up as you accumulate points against other setlist pickers.

## How to play

1. Create a free account and tonight's setlist card opens.
2. Pick Set 1 opener/closer, Set 2 opener/closer, an encore, and a wildcard.
3. Watch scores update live; climb show and tour leaderboards, or invite friends to a private pool for crew-specific standings.
