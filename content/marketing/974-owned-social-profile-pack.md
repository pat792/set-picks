## Voice (why these strings)

The bio’s job is a **click**, not a disclaimer. Lead with belonging and the night-of feeling; stack the product in fragments; end on a verb.

- **Crew** — friendly competition, play together.
- **Live** — scoring as the setlist lands; the board moves.
- **Lock** — six picks before showtime, not a claim that they play tonight.
- **Next show** — last line is a night, only the next one that exists. Do not say “play tonight” or “every night.”

Do not spend a line telling people what this isn’t. “Not a tip sheet” is a **post** gate in the cadence brief, not profile copy.

The shipped `/about` quote stays the **alternate** if the default feels too clipped on a phone. Do not combine both. Do not paste the origin story (Beaver / Glu / Andy / spreadsheet) into the bio.
